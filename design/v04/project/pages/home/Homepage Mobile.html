Give your attendees what they came for — new connections.

Step 01

Organizer promotes their Wonderelo event page

Share your Event page link — show it on slides, screens, or roll-ups using a QR code or the event hashtag.

Step 02

Participants register to rounds

People choose their time and optionally topic or group. Zero attendee setup for the organizer.

Step 03

Then they confirm attendance

An SMS notification sent 5 minutes before the round reminds them to do so.

Step 04

Participants meet & network

Wonderelo shows each participant their designated meeting point and match partners. They find each other using a unique Wonderimage™. Ice breakers handle the smooth start.

Step 05

Finally they decide whether to exchange contacts

If both parties agree, contacts are exchanged 15 minutes after the round. New contacts are made, new worlds emerge.

Features

Tailor each round to fit your event

Every event is different. Configure each networking round to match your audience, format, and goals.

Flexible duration
Choose the right length for each session — from 3-minute speed chats to hour-long deep dives.
Group size
Set anything from intimate one-on-ones to larger group brainstorms — whatever the moment calls for.
Discussion topics
Match people who share the same interests so every conversation starts on common ground.
Group matching
Great for companies. Matching within groups builds the teams. Matching across groups sparks cross-department collaboration.
Meeting points
You can use distinctive spots already in your venue — so people always know exactly where to go, no extra signage required.
Ice breakers
Optional starter questions help even the shyest attendees begin a real conversation.
Matching memory
Rounds remember past pairings — people always meet someone new.
