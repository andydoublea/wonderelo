Feature · Group matching

Make sure both sides actually mix

Sort your guests into groups, then choose whether people meet within their group or across groups. It's the one setting that turns two guest lists into one wedding.

Across groupsEvery match pairs someone from one side with someone from the other. Weddings
Within groupsPeople only meet others in their own group — handy for team-vs-team formats.

Real example: make Team Bride and Team Groom, set matching to across groups, and every round introduces one of her people to one of his — until both families feel like they've always known each other.
