Why conferences pick Wonderelo

More than a coffee break with name tags

You spent months on the talks. Don't leave the most-valuable part of the day to chance.

01

Break the bubble

Attendees meet people outside their usual circle — across industries, departments, and experience levels. The hallway conversations that "just happen" at small events finally happen at scale.

02

Zero friction

No profiles, no apps to download, no AI guessing. Scan a QR code, get matched, meet at the assigned meeting point — done. Even your most introverted attendee will play.

03

Use the venue you already have

Place printed meeting-point signs around your venue — tables, stands, roll-ups. Participants find each other at named spots. No new tech, no extra staff.

04

The moment people remember

Attendees forget which keynote opened the day. They remember the conversation that turned into a co-founder, a customer, a friend. Wonderelo creates that moment, on purpose.
