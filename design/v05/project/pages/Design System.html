01 · Identity

The wordmark

A Bricolage Grotesque wordmark, set tight and confident — all one voice, no decorative italics. The mark is a tilted square (a.k.a. our diamond), echoed in decorative dots throughout the site.

Usage rules

Purple Deep is the voice

Almost all headlines, body type and primary nav sit in Purple Deep. Reserve pure black for nothing.

Orange is the spark

One orange element per viewport — the next CTA, an italic accent word, a key icon. Never a paragraph.

Paper, not white

Default surface is Paper. White is reserved for floating tags and overlay cards that need to lift off the page.

04 · Shape

Radius, shadow, & edge

06 · Decorative

The diamonds

A 45° rotated square is Wonderelo's only proprietary mark beyond type. It punctuates corners, separates list items, and forms the wordmark's mark. Always orange or purple-deep, never grey, never inside body copy.

Sizes & colors

8 · 12 · 18 · 24 px

Pattern band

Used as a top rule for decorative section breakers — e.g. above the "As seen in" press strip.

Section corner accents

Four diamonds, scattered into the corners of long sections — at ~5–14% from each edge, sized 14–22px, alternating orange & purple. The pattern is asymmetric on purpose.

— section content lives here —

Big italic backdrop word

On Purple Deep sections, a single oversized Instrument Serif italic word bleeds off the corner at 4–8% white opacity. One word, one section.

07 · Voice

How we talk

Wonderelo's copy is warm, low-jargon, and confidently short. It addresses the host (not the guest), and it never apologizes for being lighthearted about a serious problem: people leaving events without a single conversation.

— do
  • Meet people worth knowing.
  • 9 out of 10 people leave events without a single connection.
  • Tailor each round to fit your event.
  • Three icebreakers, one room, zero awkwardness.
— don't
  • Empower attendees to forge meaningful connections.
  • Leverage our AI-powered networking platform.
  • The premier solution for enterprise event engagement.
  • Unlock authentic moments at scale.
